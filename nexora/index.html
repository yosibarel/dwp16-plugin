01 / The workflow

Draw a pattern.
Change its character.

Each of the ten tracks has its own effect chain and eight independent snapshots. Draw regions on the grid and assign a different snapshot to each pattern. One track can change character while the others keep their own sound.

01 / DRAW

Put effects in time.

Build rhythmic patterns on a tempo-synced grid. Resize either edge, move a region, or drag vertically to choose a snapshot.

02 / EXPLORE

Choose your route.

Play tracks in Parallel, Sequential, Random Step, Random Track, Selected Track or Mixdown mode. Give the same grid a different sense of movement.

03 / PERFORM

Keep it moving.

Switch master snapshots, automate playback, and balance tracks with Mix Pan, Mix and Output controls.

02 / The effects

Eight snapshots.
For every track.

Every track has its own set of eight effect snapshots, independent of the other tracks. Shape different variations of its chain, then let grid patterns recall them — or choose a random snapshot each time a pattern plays.

Keep eight versions of each track’s effect settings ready to play. Give every pattern a snapshot, edit a variation in place, or let Random choose the next one. Master snapshots shape the final chain separately.
