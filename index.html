START WITH THE DETAILS

Shape the sound.
Keep the character.

Set a loop, reverse a phrase or draw pitch and gain curves directly over your sample. Use FM to add a new edge, then render the result into fresh audio with Process Sample.

EIGHT PATTERNS. SEVEN WAYS TO PLAY.

One pattern.
Or a conversation.

Play a single pattern, chain a sequence, build a playlist, explore random and shuffle orders, move back and forth, or run patterns in parallel. Quantized launch and retrigger controls help each change land in time.

CHANNEL FX. MASTER FX. SNAPSHOTS.

Change the texture.
Move the whole sound.

Build effect chains for individual channels and the master. Slice with Shuffle, shape rhythm with Gate, or add space and tone with Delay and Filter. Store variations in snapshots and switch them from the sequencer or glitch engine; enable Auto in the plug-in to select snapshots from host automation.

CONTROLLED DISRUPTION

Keep the groove.
Change what happens inside.

Move between four glitch variations with their own beat, length, pitch, reverse, pan and gain settings. Weight the choices, switch effect snapshots and reshape a phrase while it plays. Choose Glitch for captured-audio playback or FX Only to switch snapshots over live audio, then shape the result with Mix, Mix Pan and Fade.

SEE EVERY CONNECTION

Make movement.
Keep it connected.

Route LFOs, sequencer curves, MIDI and XY controls to parameters across the instrument. The modulation manager puts sources, targets, depth and direction in one searchable view, with add, edit, remove and undo controls.

PLAY. RECORD. USE IT AGAIN.

Your performance.
Your next sample.

Record your performance, capture a sidechain input in a compatible DAW, or use an external input in Standalone. Export the recording or drag it into a channel to keep building from the result.
