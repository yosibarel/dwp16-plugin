VST3 / macOS + WINDOWS01

A focused MIDI workspace.

Load Sequencer as a VST3 MIDI effect in a compatible host and route its MIDI output to your instrument. The full pattern editor is always at hand.

MIDI routing depends on your DAW. A host and an instrument are required; Sequencer is not a standalone sound generator.

MAX FOR LIVE02

Make your Live set move.

Place the included MIDI device before an instrument in Ableton Live. Open the editor from the device and map CTRL 1–4 to parameters in the next instrument or effect.

Requires Ableton Live with Max for Live and the installed Sequencer VST3. The Max device hosts the same plug-in.
