BEFORE YOU START

A few useful details.

Does Sequencer make sound on its own?

No. Sequencer generates MIDI for your instrument. Route its MIDI output to an instrument in your DAW, or place the Max for Live device before an instrument in Live.

Do I need Max for Live?

Only for the included Ableton Live device. The VST3 plug-in can be used independently in a compatible host. The Max device requires the VST3 to be installed.

How does the trial work?

The trial provides ten minutes per DAW session, shared by Sequencer instances in that process. Saving presets and Sequencer project state is disabled until activation. See the trial guide for details.

Where can I get help?

Read the user guide or email dwp@dwp-plugin.com with your operating system, DAW version and a description of the issue.
